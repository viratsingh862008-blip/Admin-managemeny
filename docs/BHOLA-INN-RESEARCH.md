# Hotel Bhola Inn — Research Report

Research snapshot: 4 October 2026.

Hotel Bhola Inn is listed at Station Chowk, Supriya Cinema Road, Bettiah, West Champaran, Bihar 845438. Goibibo describes it as a 3-star hotel and about an 8-minute walk from Bettiah Railway Station. It lists Deluxe and Suite rooms; Deluxe is about 200 sq ft with a king bed and one bathroom, while Suite is about 400 sq ft with a king bed and one bathroom.

Public amenity listings include restaurant, room service, air conditioning, free public parking, laundry, free Wi-Fi, luggage assistance, toiletries, mineral water, iron/ironing board, fire extinguishers and CCTV. Aggregators disagree on some policy times, so these should remain editable in Admin and be verified by the hotel.

MakeMyTrip currently shows 15 property/guest photos, the Deluxe/Suite room types and a 3.8/5 snapshot across 33 ratings. Recent review snippets include both positives (spacious/neat rooms, restaurant/service, pleasant family stay) and negatives (internet availability, geyser heating and bed cleanliness).

Restaurant Guru currently shows 36 photos and a Google-derived 3.8/5 snapshot from 474 votes. It shows restaurant features including takeaway, delivery, dinner, breakfast, birthday parties, good service and location. Its public menu page does not expose a dependable official item list, so this build does not invent menu items or prices.

The public search index did not provide a confidently verified official Instagram or Facebook account. A YouTube Short titled "Hotel Bhola inn bettiah || sweets & pure veg restaurant" by Vlogger Bihari BR22 demonstrates third-party video coverage, not official ownership.

A directory currently lists www.hotelbholainn.com as the website field and an older Google Business Site URL. Both should be verified with the hotel before making them canonical.
